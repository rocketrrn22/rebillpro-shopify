Why Selling Plans?

Shopify only saves a customer's payment method when they buy through a Selling Plan at checkout. Without one, no card gets vaulted and subscription billing won't work.
Setup flow: Create a plan below → assign it to your products in Shopify admin → customers subscribe at checkout → card is saved → you can rebill anytime.
